# Yardscout — Pricing & Target Customer

## Pricing (current)

| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| Setup / onboarding | **Free** |
| First month | **$49 / rep** |
| Standard, month 2+ | **$89 / rep / mo** (**$74 / rep** billed annually) |
| Pause & restart | **Free** |
| Seat minimum | none |

**Example (3-rep shop):** ~$147 month one, then ~$267/mo. No setup fee.

### Why these numbers
- **Per-seat, not commission** (manufactured-home commission rules + attribution problems make a cut of the sale legally/practically messy).
- **$89/seat** sits above generic canvassing tools ($49–75) because the fit-map + AR are deal-closing features no competitor combines; value anchor = one extra placement is worth thousands, so a seat is a rounding error *if it lifts close rate*.
- **$49 first month** = low-friction entry to prove value before full price.
- **No setup fee** — keeps the door wide open for the first dealers; onboarding is part of landing the logo, not a line item. (Removed the earlier $299 setup.)
- **Free pause/restart** — the business is seasonal; charging to come back punishes the loyal customer. (Killed the earlier $200 reactivation fee — simulated buyers were unanimous against it.)

## Target customer (ICP)

The dividing line is **mindset, not age.**

**Buy (target):**
- Manufactured-home / ADU / backyard-placement dealers (or adjacent door-knocking trades) with a **crew of ~2–10 reps** — big enough that shared data + pre-qualified doors matter.
- **Growth-minded** (scaling crews, wants to look professional at the door) **or ROI-driven** (kill wasted drives, measure rep output).
- Tech-comfortable: already on a smartphone, maybe a CRM or ads. *Reaching for* tools.
- **Trigger:** adding crews (needs shared data) or feeling the pain of wasted drives / weak door pitches.
- Skews **mid-30s to ~50s**, but treat age as a loose correlate — a growth-minded 55-year-old buys; a set-in-his-ways 35-year-old doesn't.

**Don't chase:**
- The entrenched paper-and-gut veteran who believes no app can tell him anything. Not an early customer.

**First customer:** Gavin's dad — a veteran ("been doing it a while") who is *actively asking for software to make it easier*, which makes him a motivated pragmatist (not a holdout), plus a warm/friend relationship. Ideal first logo: land him, prove one extra placement, sell that proof to the next growth/ROI operator.

### Simulated-buyer read (3 personas)
- Growth/tech operator (Marcus-type): **7/10** — fit-map is "the product," AR is a closing weapon.
- ROI pragmatist (Dana-type): **6/10** — buys after proof; wants accuracy numbers, data export, vendor-risk cover.
- Old-school veteran (Hank-type): **3/10** — not the market.
- Unanimous fixes surfaced: **kill the reactivation fee (done), publish/flag fit accuracy, give data export, keep offline on the roadmap.**

> Note: simulated personas are a thinking tool, not validation. Real confidence = putting it in front of actual dealers, starting with Gavin's dad.
