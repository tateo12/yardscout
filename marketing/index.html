Fit & legal scoring

It knows the setbacks before your rep does.

Every city on the Wasatch Front writes its own rules. Yardscout carries the real ones per municipality and checks each lot against them automatically.

  • Minimum lot size, side and rear setbacks, and how far behind the house a unit has to sit — per city, not a rough guess.
  • Size caps applied as a share of the primary home so the footprint it draws is one the owner could actually permit.
  • A real-scale unit dropped into the backyard with the clearances left visible, so fit is obvious at a glance.

Lead intelligence

The best door isn't the biggest yard. It's the owner who can say yes.

Yardscout reads live county assessor records, so your rep knows who they're about to talk to before they knock.

  • Owner-occupied vs. investor — skip the rentals where nobody at the door can decide.
  • How long they've owned — long tenure usually means built-up equity to finance against.
  • Assessed home value — a quick read on the equity there is to borrow from.

See it in their yard

The pitch they can't picture, made real on the spot.

The hardest part of the sale is getting a homeowner to imagine a second home behind their house. Yardscout just shows them.

  • A real-scale 3D model dropped onto their lot's satellite view — sized to the footprint that actually fits.
  • Phone AR to place the unit through the camera so they can stand in the yard and walk around it.
  • A catalog of real floor plans — single- and double-wides with true dimensions, not stock renders.
